Call Blocker++ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Call Blocker++ is a call-blocking app for iPhone, made by Devendra Satram. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the developer does not collect, receive, share or sell any of your personal data.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • The numbers and ranges you block. They are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and handed to iOS through Apple's Call Directory extension so that iOS can block calls from them. They are never sent to the developer or to any server.
  • Your settings, such as the country used to read the numbers you type.

The app does not access your Contacts, call history, location, microphone or any other personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

Full Access is an optional one-time in-app purchase, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, Apple ID, payment details or address. On your device, the app only asks Apple whether Full Access has been bought, so it can unlock it. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time.

Deleting your data

You can remove any blocked number or range in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
